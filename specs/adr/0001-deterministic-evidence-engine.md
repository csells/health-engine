# Keep Health Engine deterministic and document interpretation outside it

The Health Engine will own the private Workspace data model and storage interfaces, deterministic interpretation, evidence policy, reference-data lifecycle, and versioned structured analyses across genomics and other typed Health Facts. Model-based document reading, interviews, and narrative synthesis remain in skills and applications. Agents and applications extract facts from PDFs, screenshots, free text, and conversations, then write those structured facts—with provenance and uncertainty—through the engine. This seam lets every caller share one validated health record and interpretation implementation without putting personal health data, document-understanding behavior, or agent behavior in the reusable OSS repository.
