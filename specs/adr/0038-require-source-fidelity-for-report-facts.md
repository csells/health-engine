# Require origin-appropriate assurance for facts used in reports

Evidence Assurance follows origin rather than pretending every fact can be Source-verified. Machine-readable Sources are parser-validated through deterministic parsers and independent regression fixtures. Document-extracted facts become source-verified only after a separate comparison to an exact Source location recording verifier, method, version, time, scope, and result. A direct Subject or Operator statement is an explicitly attributed self-report, not Verification of a document or of clinical truth. Parser-validated and source-verified evidence may support established Findings; self-report may do so only with visible attribution. Unverified evidence is unresolved, and quarantined, conflicting, or hazardous evidence cannot silently trigger Guidance. Any Guidance use of self-report requires the cited rule to accept it for that prerequisite. Evidence Assurance remains distinct from Analysis Confidence.
