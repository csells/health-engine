# Surface known errors in the medical record

Health Engine will store known or credibly suspected chart errors as first-class Record Hazards linked to the affected Sources and Health Facts. A hazard preserves the problematic statement, why it is wrong or dangerous, the corrected understanding, supporting evidence, author, time, and verification state. It does not alter the original Source. Queries, Analyses, and renderers involving the affected condition, medication, symptom, or event must surface the hazard so downstream agents and reports do not copy the known error forward as truth.
