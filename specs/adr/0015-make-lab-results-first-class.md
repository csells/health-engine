# Make Lab Results first-class Health Facts

The Health Engine will accept structured Lab Results and analyze them longitudinally. Each result preserves its collection date or time, original test name, numeric or qualitative value, units, the reporting lab's own reference range and flag, and its source reference. The engine may identify trends, reversals, abnormal results, and stale measurements, but it will not substitute a different lab's range or flatten “not measured,” “not found,” and “normal” into the same state. Lab-document extraction remains the caller's responsibility.
