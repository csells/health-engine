# Make Vital Measurements first-class Health Facts

Health Engine will store and analyze dated Vital Measurements including weight, blood pressure, pulse, temperature, oxygen saturation, and extensible future measurements. Each fact preserves its value, units, time, setting, observer or device when known, source, and relevant context such as fasting state, symptoms, or medication dose. The engine may calculate longitudinal trends and meaningful changes while keeping unlike contexts—such as home and clinic blood pressure—visible rather than silently treating every reading as interchangeable.
