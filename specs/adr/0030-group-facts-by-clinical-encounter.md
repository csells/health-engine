# Group related Health Facts by Clinical Encounter

Health Engine will store doctor visits, emergency visits, hospitalizations, consultations, and similar events as Clinical Encounters. An encounter records its date or interval, type, provider, facility, reason, and Sources, and groups the observations, Condition Assertions, Medication Events, procedures, orders, Care Tasks, and other decisions extracted from it. The grouped facts remain independently sourced and queryable; the encounter supplies context without replacing them with a prose summary.
